SOLMAR

Sun, sea, and trouble — an open-world crime game

Riko Vane came to Solmar with a dead car and a live debt. Mara Solano, the fixer who runs the city's quieter errands, has work for a driver she can trust. Steal a ride, outrun the Solmar PD, run the jobs — and take the city for yourself.

Drive / walk: WASD or arrows  ·  Run: Shift
Enter / exit car: E  ·  Handbrake: Space
Shoot: Click (hold for auto)  ·  Weapons: 1 pistol, 2 SMG, 3 shotgun  ·  Punch: F  ·  Camera: drag mouse
Next job: M  ·  Save: P  ·  Sound: N  ·  Pause: Esc

An original game. Solmar, Riko Vane, Mara Solano and the Solmar PD are fictional — no Rockstar Games content. Runs fully offline.
